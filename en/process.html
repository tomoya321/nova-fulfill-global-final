Process

A clear workflow from first brief to global shipping.

Every project is different, but the operating logic stays transparent.

01

Brief & Requirements

We clarify product details, target costs, quality standards, packaging, destination markets and expected volume.

  • Product links, photos or specifications
  • MOQ and target pricing
  • Branding and packaging requirements
02

Sourcing & Samples

We find or review suppliers, compare quotations and organize samples when needed.

  • Supplier comparison
  • Sample sourcing
  • Cost and feasibility review
03

Order & Production Coordination

Once approved, we coordinate purchase details, timelines and critical production requirements.

04

QC & Receiving

Goods are checked to the agreed inspection scope, documented and received into inventory.

05

Branding & Fulfillment

Orders are picked, branded, packed and shipped through suitable international channels.

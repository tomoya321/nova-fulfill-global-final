EXCEPTION PLAYBOOK

What happens when something goes wrong?

A mature workflow defines the decision before urgency takes over. No rework, replacement or dispatch should proceed without evidence and a named approval.

01 / RECORD

Document the issue

Photos, video, SKU, quantity and inspection context establish a shared factual record.

02 / CONTAIN

Separate affected stock

Questionable units are held back so they do not enter good inventory or customer orders.

03 / DECIDE

Present practical options

Rework, replacement, discount, partial approval or rejection are compared with cost and timing impact.

04 / CLOSE

Confirm and prevent

The approved action is recorded, then supplier and QC instructions are updated for the next batch.

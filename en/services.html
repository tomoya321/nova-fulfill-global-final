01

Product sourcing

Start with a 1688 or Alibaba link, photo, sample or specification. We compare suitable suppliers and clarify price, MOQ, lead time and customization.

  • Supplier search and shortlisting
  • Quote, MOQ and lead-time comparison
  • Sample coordination
  • Local supplier communication in Chinese
02

Quality control

Catch visible defects, wrong SKUs and packaging problems before stock is released for fulfillment.

  • Quantity and SKU verification
  • Appearance and packaging inspection
  • Dimension or basic function checks
  • Photo/video evidence and QC notes
03

Warehousing & inventory

Receive goods from multiple suppliers, organize them by SKU and keep stock close to packaging and fulfillment.

  • Inbound receiving and SKU registration
  • Inventory storage and organization
  • Supplier consolidation
  • Bundle and fulfillment preparation
04

Customization & private label

Turn generic sourced products into a more coherent branded experience.

  • Logo and label coordination
  • Custom boxes, pouches and mailers
  • Cards, manuals and inserts
  • Kitting, bundling and OEM coordination
05

Order fulfillment

Pick, pack and dispatch customer orders from China with tracking and brand-specific packing rules.

  • Pick & pack by SKU/order
  • Branded packaging materials
  • Shipment tracking
  • Route options by destination
06

Supplier consolidation

If you already have suppliers, use us as the local operational layer between those vendors and your overseas customers.

  • Receive from multiple factories
  • Centralized QC and packaging
  • Single inventory location
  • One point of responsibility in China
